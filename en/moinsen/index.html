Regional cuisine right by the water

Moin’sen serves local cuisine with modern touches: ingredients come wherever possible from regional suppliers and producers, freshly prepared in a family atmosphere. There is also a selection of beers and cocktails. Guests with dogs are welcome – four-legged friends are expressly invited. The kitchen cooks daily until 8 pm.

Location and how to get there

You will find Moin’sen in a prime spot on the Priwall, right by the ferry landing. From the old-town side of Travemünde it is an easy hop across the Trave on the Priwall ferry; by car it is at Mecklenburger Landstraße 1. Whether a large Baltic ferry, a sailing yacht or a motorboat – from the seat by the water you can watch the comings and goings on the Trave.

Frequently asked questions about Moin’sen

Where is Moin’sen located in Travemünde?

Moin’sen im Fährhaus is on the Priwall, right by the ferry landing on the Trave (Mecklenburger Landstraße 1, 23570 Lübeck-Travemünde). From the Travemünde side you can reach it on the Priwall ferry.

What are the opening hours of Moin’sen?

Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday from 3 pm, Saturday from 2 pm, Sunday from 12 noon, each until 9:30 pm. Tuesday is the closing day. The kitchen cooks daily until 8 pm.

Can you reserve a table at Moin’sen?

Yes. Moin’sen takes table reservations by phone on +49 170 189 50 51 during opening hours.

Are dogs allowed at Moin’sen?

Yes, dogs are expressly welcome at Moin’sen.

What kind of cuisine does Moin’sen offer?

Regional cuisine with modern touches, freshly prepared from ingredients that are as local as possible, plus a selection of beers and cocktails.
