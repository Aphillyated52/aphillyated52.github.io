Travemünde is the Baltic seaside resort of Lübeck: fine sandy beach at the mouth of the Trave, the Old Lighthouse, the four-masted barque Passat and Travemünde Week – one of the largest sailing regattas in the world. This page brings together what matters today: the official bathing-water rating, the current water temperature, today’s events and live departures. Every fact is backed by a source and a visible verification date.

Frequently asked questions about Travemünde

What can you do in Travemünde today?

This page delivers the daily overview: today’s events, water temperature and bathing-water status, live train and bus departures and the weather. For a ready-made day plan with a route there is the coast planner.

How warm is the water in Travemünde?

The current, measured Baltic water temperature is in the live ticker above and in detail on the water-temperature page – with timestamp, day preview and official bathing-water status. The page updates several times a day.

When is Travemünde Week 2026?

The 137th Travemünde Week runs from 17 to 26 July 2026 – regattas on the Bay of Lübeck and a shore programme, entry to the festival area is free. Getting there, parking and tips are on the Travemünde Week page.

How do you get to the Priwall?

By the Priwall ferry across the Trave: a car ferry takes vehicles, the passenger ferry brings pedestrians and cyclists across in a few minutes all year round – to the beach, the Passat, the marine station and the nature reserve.

Is Travemünde heute an official site?

No – Travemünde heute is an independent, private information service and not a site of the city of Lübeck or the resort administration. Every detail carries a source and verification date; the methodology page explains how it works.

Both of Travemünde's official bathing sites have held the top EU class for years. Travemünde main beach (Kurstrand): rated “excellent” in all 14 EU assessment years since 2012. Priwall beach: “excellent” in 12 of 15 EU assessment years, otherwise “good” – “excellent” without interruption since 2021.
