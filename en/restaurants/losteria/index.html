The restaurant

According to the website the menu offers crispy pizzas, home-made pasta, antipasti and dolci as well as selected wines. There is a sun terrace with Baltic flair, plus reservation, collection and delivery – also to holiday flats. Lunch offers and children’s dishes round out the range. The TA.SH tourism data list the house as a location of the Italian chain, with starters, salads, pasta, pizza and meat dishes. Am Leuchtenfeld lies between the old town and the spa quarter, less than a hundred metres from the old lighthouse – so unlike the houses on the Vorderreihe it is not on the Trave but on the beach side of town. Per OpenStreetMap there are outdoor seats, vegetarian and vegan dishes and Wi-Fi. According to the website the house opens at midday only at weekends; on weekdays service starts at 3 pm.

Frequently asked questions about L’Osteria

What does L’Osteria in Travemünde offer?

Italian cuisine with crispy pizzas, home-made pasta, antipasti and dolci as well as selected wines.

When is L’Osteria open?

According to the website Monday to Friday 3–10 pm and Saturday to Sunday 12–10 pm.

Can you have it delivered or collect it?

Yes, according to the website reservation, collection and delivery – also to holiday flats – are possible.

How far is it from L’Osteria to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 100 m as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 600 m to Travemünde Strand station and about 1 km to the Priwall ferry.
