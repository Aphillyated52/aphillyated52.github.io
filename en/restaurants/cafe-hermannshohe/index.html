The restaurant

High above the sea on the cliff in the protected landscape between Travemünde and Niendorf, the Hermannshöhe serves breakfast, home-made cakes from its own bakery and changing lunch dishes including fish and North-German classics. The self-service format keeps prices moderate and waits short. Families find an outdoor area with a playground; in the evening the house serves as an event venue. The TA.SH tourism data describe the kitchen as traditional Holstein cooking with fish dishes and North-German classics. The opening details differ between sources: the website gives 11 am to 7 pm daily, OpenStreetMap lists seasonal hours with an earlier start at weekends – worth checking the website before setting out. It is the only address in this guide outside the town itself, a good three kilometres north of the beach promenade on top of the cliff; on foot the way leads along the shore path towards Niendorf.

Frequently asked questions about Café Hermannshöhe

What exactly is the Hermannshöhe?

A self-service café with a sea view, high above the sea on the Travemünde cliff; in the evening it serves as an event venue.

Is there breakfast?

Yes, a breakfast buffet is offered at the weekend according to tourism information – best to check the current times in advance.

When is it open?

According to the website the café is open daily from 11 am to 7 pm.

How far is it from Café Hermannshöhe to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 3 km as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 2.5 km to Travemünde Strand station and about 3.6 km to the Priwall ferry.
