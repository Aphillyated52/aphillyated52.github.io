The restaurant

The cuisine combines Italian tradition with Mediterranean dishes, fresh fish and regional ingredients from northern Germany. It offers antipasti, pasta, pizza, meat and fish dishes, home-made desserts and a grappa selection, plus daily changing recommendations from the fresh catch. The restaurant opened in May 2020 and spans two floors. The TA.SH tourism data give pasta, pizza and seasonal dishes such as fried scallops as examples and highlight the wine list with Italian and German wines as well as the home-made tiramisu. The phone numbers differ between sources: the restaurant website gives 04502 8885645, while directories also list the older number 04502 3640 – the operator entry applies here. According to the website the kitchen closes half an hour before the restaurant itself.

Frequently asked questions about Ristorante Due Fratelli

What cuisine does the Due Fratelli offer?

Italian-Mediterranean cuisine with fresh fish and regional ingredients; the menu includes antipasti, pasta, pizza and meat and fish dishes.

Where is the Due Fratelli?

At Vorderreihe 17, where the Trave meets the Baltic.

When is the Due Fratelli open?

According to the website daily from 12 noon to 10 pm, the kitchen until 9:30 pm.

How far is it from Ristorante Due Fratelli to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 900 m as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 1.1 km to Travemünde Strand station and about 100 m to the Priwall ferry.
