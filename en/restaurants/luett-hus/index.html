The restaurant

On the Vorderreihe promenade, the Lütt Hus stands for authentic North-German fish dishes and fresh, maritime home cooking. The small house is one of the well-known fish addresses in town. No official website could be found; the details come from tourism and dining directories. The TA.SH tourism data list the house as a small, fine fish house on the Vorderreihe promenade; the phone number and hours there match the entries in OpenStreetMap, both giving Tuesday to Sunday from 12 noon. Outdoor seats are recorded in OpenStreetMap – in good weather that means sitting on the promenade where walkers pass between the ferry pier and the old lighthouse. It is only a few hundred metres from here to the Priwall ferry pier. According to the same sources, Monday is the closing day.

Frequently asked questions about Lütt Hus

What kind of restaurant is the Lütt Hus?

A small fish house on the Travemünde Vorderreihe with North-German fish dishes and maritime home cooking.

Where is the Lütt Hus?

At Vorderreihe 25b, right on the beach promenade.

When is it open?

Monday is the closing day; best to confirm the other times by phone in advance, as directory listings differ.

What sets the Lütt Hus apart?

A small fish house right on the Vorderreihe promenade – per the TA.SH tourism data with authentic North-German fish dishes and maritime home cooking.

How far is it from Lütt Hus to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 800 m as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 1 km to Travemünde Strand station and about 150 m to the Priwall ferry.
