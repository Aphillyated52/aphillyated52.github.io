The restaurant

The house describes itself as “modern cuisine in historic dress” and, since mid-2014, uses various areas of the Alte Vogtei with indoor and outdoor seating, each with its own charm. It offers a varied, regularly changing selection of fish dishes, meals, cakes and drinks. Reservations are also possible via WhatsApp. The TA.SH tourism data list pasta, meat dishes and cakes alongside fish and highlight the regularly changing menu as well as freshly caught fish from the nearby Baltic. The Alte Vogtei at Vorderreihe 7 is one of the historic houses in the old town; the restaurant uses several areas inside it. The address is at the western end of the Vorderreihe, a few steps from the Priwall ferry – the shortest way to the peninsula starts outside the door.

Frequently asked questions about Fisch und Meer
