The restaurant

On the Priwall promenade, where large fishing boats enter the Bay of Lübeck, the Ahoi menu ranges from fish and chips to sushi bowls, tuna sashimi and shrimp dishes and currywurst – under the motto “The main thing is tasty!”. It is the second Ahoi location and sits right by the water. Online ordering and reservation are possible, and dogs are welcome. The TA.SH tourism data name the Hamburg brothers Steffen and Peter Henssler as the operators, who opened the Ahoi in Travemünde in 2019, and list German classics such as currywurst and schnitzel Henssler style alongside sushi bowls and fish and chips. The large outdoor terrace sits where the Trave flows into the Bay of Lübeck. The address belongs to the Priwall – from the town centre the way leads across the Priwall ferry or the long way round via Mecklenburger Landstraße.

Frequently asked questions about Ahoi Steffen Henssler

What is on the menu at Ahoi?

Among others fish and chips, sushi bowls, tuna sashimi, shrimp dishes and currywurst – under the motto “The main thing is tasty!”.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, dogs are welcome at Ahoi.

When is it open?

According to the website daily from 12 noon to 10 pm, the kitchen until 9 pm.

How far is it from Ahoi Steffen Henssler to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 350 m as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 850 m to Travemünde Strand station and about 1.1 km to the Priwall ferry. The way into town goes via the Priwall ferry.
