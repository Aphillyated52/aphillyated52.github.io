The restaurant

In a rustic ambience and family atmosphere, the Olympos serves classic Greek dishes. It is within walking distance of the beach promenade and spa park. Reservations are only possible by phone. The TA.SH tourism data describe the Olympos as a small house with rustic furnishings serving classic Greek dishes, and note explicitly that reservations are taken by phone only. According to the same data it opens in two blocks, at midday and in the evening; on Wednesdays it stays closed. Kurgartenstraße links the old town with the spa quarter – so the Olympos sits on the way between the Vorderreihe and the beach promenade, without being on the water itself. The Olympos does not run a website of its own; the phone number and hours come from the TA.SH tourism data and from OpenStreetMap, which differ from each other on the opening times.

Frequently asked questions about Olympos

What cuisine does the Olympos offer?

Classic Greek dishes.

How can you make a reservation?

Reservations are only possible by phone.

Where is the Olympos?

At Kurgartenstraße 52, within walking distance of the beach promenade and spa park.

When is the Olympos open?

Per the TA.SH tourism data, midday from 11 am to 2 pm and evening from 5 to 10 pm, closed on Wednesdays. Without guarantee – call ahead.

How far is it from Olympos to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 800 m as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 1 km to Travemünde Strand station and about 200 m to the Priwall ferry.
