The restaurant

From the CasaGrande you look out freely over the harbour scene and the Priwall. The cuisine is Italian with fine specialities, regional fish and seafood dishes and selected wines; lamb fillet is among the dishes mentioned. The restaurant also suits business dinners and family celebrations according to its own description. The TA.SH tourism data list the CasaGrande as an Italian restaurant right at the fishing harbour and highlight the glass frontage, the terrace and the wine selection. The fishing harbour at Baggersand is the working harbour of the town: the cutters set out from here and their catch is sold in Travemünde – at this address the short way from quay to kitchen is geography rather than a figure of speech. Tuesday is the closing day; on the other days, according to the website, service runs from midday through to the evening.

Frequently asked questions about Ristorante CasaGrande

Where is the CasaGrande?

At Auf dem Baggersand 7 on the Priwall, right by the Travemünde fishing harbour.

What cuisine does the CasaGrande offer?

Italian cuisine with regional fish and seafood dishes and selected wines.

When is the CasaGrande open?

Tuesday is the closing day; from Wednesday to Monday the kitchen serves continuously from 12 noon to 10 pm.

How far is it from Ristorante CasaGrande to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 1.1 km as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 1.3 km to Travemünde Strand station and about 200 m to the Priwall ferry.
