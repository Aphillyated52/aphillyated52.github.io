The restaurant

According to public information the menu comprises around 120 different dishes, including classics such as gyros, souvlaki, lamb dishes, fish and meze as well as moussaka; there are also vegetarian and vegan options. A home-made soup is served before the lunch dishes. The TA.SH tourism data describe the Kymata as a small, family-run house with traditional Greek cooking and explicitly name vegetarian specialities and home-made desserts alongside fish and meat dishes. According to the same data it opens in two separate blocks, at midday and in the evening; in the afternoon the kitchen stays cold. The restaurant sits on the narrow street Rose away from the waterfront promenade – quieter than the addresses right on the Trave, but without a water view. On Mondays the restaurant stays closed according to all available sources.

Frequently asked questions about Restaurant Kymata

What cuisine does Kymata offer?

Greek cuisine, freshly prepared, with dishes such as gyros, souvlaki, lamb, fish and meze.

Are there vegetarian or vegan dishes?

Yes, the menu also includes vegetarian and vegan options according to public information.

Where is Kymata?

On the street Rose 7 in Lübeck-Travemünde.

How far is it from Restaurant Kymata to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 650 m as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 800 m to Travemünde Strand station and about 400 m to the Priwall ferry.
