The restaurant

At the start of the Trave promenade, GOSCH serves freshly prepared maritime specialities Sylt style, along with wines and snacks to take away. There is a large sun terrace and a bar with a view of the four-masted barque Passat and the passing ships. At weekends there is regular live music according to the operator. The TA.SH tourism data single out the mussels, both in broth and in maritime soups, and list the house as a fish restaurant at the start of the Trave promenade with a view of the water and the passing ships. GOSCH began as a brand from Sylt, where its fish counters became well known; the Travemünde operation is one of its mainland locations. Anyone just pausing on the promenade walk will find the counter for takeaway alongside the restaurant service.

Frequently asked questions about GOSCH Travemünde

What kind of restaurant is GOSCH Travemünde?

A fish outlet of the Sylt brand GOSCH with maritime specialities Sylt style, right on the Trave promenade.

Is there food to take away?

Yes, besides the terrace service there are snacks to take away.

When is it open?

According to the website it is open daily from 11 am – best to check the exact closing time in advance.

How far is it from GOSCH Travemünde to the beach and the station?

It is roughly 400 m as the crow flies to the beach promenade at the old lighthouse, about 650 m to Travemünde Strand station and about 600 m to the Priwall ferry.
